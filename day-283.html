Day 283 — The Permanent Measure Alone

"The measure that remains binding is the one the man applies when every collective measure of higher order has disappeared." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

The permanent measure alone continues after every collective measure of higher order has been dissolved. The residual man continues to apply the permanent standard even though the age offers no confirming authority and no shared criterion. The measure itself remains intact. Only the collective structures that once enforced it have disappeared.

Young men who still seek a reliable standard often look for residual authorities or residual communities that will supply the measure from outside. Most of those authorities have already adjusted the standard until it no longer differs from the horizontal demands of the age. The residual path does not wait for external confirmation. It continues to apply the permanent measure from the interior center that still recognizes its force.

This solitary application is the residual form of the older traditional measure that once ordered both the individual and the civilization. The man who keeps the measure present already performs the residual act. That act does not require a collective criterion to remain real.

Apply the permanent measure alone. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions in which higher standards are publicly enforced. The residual act is to keep the permanent measure effective in every concrete decision. That effectiveness is the continuity of the higher type under residual conditions.
