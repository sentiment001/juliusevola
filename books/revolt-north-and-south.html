North and South

“Generally speaking, we can distinguish between a Southern and a Northern Light; in the amount in which such an opposition has relatively well-defined traits within the mixed matter of what is historical and traceable back to very distant ages, it is possible to distinguish between a Uranian and a lunar spirituality, between ‘Arctic regions’ and ‘Atlantis.’” Revolt Against the Modern World

This is a very general and yet fundamental scheme. One can speak, in a universal sense, of the Light of the South and the Light of the North. In the measure in which that opposition can have a relatively precise meaning in the mixed matter of what is temporal and which moreover goes back to quite remote epochs, one can also speak of Uranian spirituality and of lunar spirituality, of “Arctida” and of “Atlantis.” Historically and geographically, Atlantis would correspond in reality not to the South but to the West. To the South would correspond Lemuria, already referred to in passing, of which certain Negroid and southern populations can be considered as the last vestiges. Since until now the trajectory of the decline of the primordial Hyperborean civilization has essentially been followed, Atlantis is considered only in reference to one phase of this decline. Moreover, the South is considered only in relation to the influence it exercised on the races of origin and of Boreal civilization during the Atlantic cycle (but not only in it, if one does not give to this expression “Atlantic” a generalized and typological sense), within intermediate forms that have a double meaning: an alteration of the primordial heritage and an elevation to purer forms of the chthonic-demonic themes proper to the aboriginal southern races. For this reason the term South is not used, but Light of the South, and for the second cycle the expression “lunar spirituality” will be employed, the Moon being considered as a luminous but non-solar symbol, similar in a certain way to a “heavenly Earth,” that is, to a purified land (South).

The Northern Light is the immutable solar principle, the virile and warrior ethic, the Olympian and polar orientation that does not derive from the contingent. It is the light of the Hyperborean origins, retained in greater purity by the stocks that maintained a more direct relation to the original seat or that rekindled its fire even in distant environments. The Southern Light is the principle of mutation, of the cyclical nature of life, of the Mother and the Earth, the pantheistic and contemplative ethos that absorbs the individual into the generative and telluric element. The swastika, as a rotary movement around an immovable center, belongs to the northern stage: the sun as constant and unchanging source of light. The southern forms emphasize the generative and maternal, the lunar reflection rather than the solar source. The decline of the primordial Hyperborean civilization is marked by a shift toward mixed spiritual forms that incorporate southern influences. In this degenerative phase the Mother and lunar spirituality gain prominence over the sun-centered, warrior ethos. The Atlantean tradition opened a new cycle, the Silver Age, a mixture which already has the sense of degeneration of North and South.

From north to south and from west to east, through diffusion, adaptation, or domination, there arose civilizations that originally shared, to a certain degree, the same matrix, and often strains of the same spiritual legacy found in the conquering elites. Yet the contact with the powers of the South conditioned the original Uranian orientation. The red-skinned race of the last inhabitants of Atlantis, those who according to Plato forfeited their pristine divine nature through repeated unions with the human race, corresponds to this intermediate condition. These people are regarded as the original ethnic stock of several newer civilizations established by the migratory waves from west to east (the red race of the Cretan-Aegeans, Eteocretans, Pelasgians, Lycians, Egyptians, Kefti, and others) and of the American civilizations. The Northern Light was considerably lost to the Atlantean offshoot which defiled itself through spiritual integration into the spiritual lunar sphere of the world of the Mother or Earth of the Southern Light and further miscegenation with inferior stocks. World-history presents itself as the saga of the dualistic conflict between the Northern Light and the Southern Light: on one side the Uranian, patriarchal stocks of purer Hyperborean lineage; on the other the chthonic and titanized inferior races and the spiritually and ethnically mixed heirs of the fallen Atlantean civilization captured by the Southern Light.

The demand the chapter places on the reader is therefore exact. North and South are not merely geographical. They are the fundamental polarity of two lights and two spiritualities: the Uranian and polar against the lunar and maternal, the immutable solar principle against the generative and telluric. Every later civilization is the dynamic product of the meeting or collision of these tendencies. To recognize the distinction is to hold the higher measure of the Northern Light against which the mixture, the lunar conditioning, and the progressive intrusion of the Southern element are judged. Without that recognition the subsequent cycles remain unintelligible, and the revolt against the modern world has no original polarity by which to orient itself.
