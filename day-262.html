Day 262 — Form Without Collective Support

"Form of a higher order survives the collapse of every external support when it is maintained as an interior reality by the man who still lives according to the permanent criteria." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

Form without collective support continues after every external structure that once sustained higher form has been leveled. The residual man continues to maintain form according to the permanent measure even though the age offers no confirming framework and no shared recognition. The form itself remains intact. Only the collective vehicles that once carried it have disappeared.

Young men who still sense the need for form often look for residual institutions or residual methods that will supply it from outside. Most of those institutions have already been reshaped by the same forces that dissolved the older forms. The residual path does not wait for external support. It continues to maintain form from the interior center that still perceives the permanent criteria.

This solitary form is the residual expression of the older traditional shape that once ordered both the individual and the higher functions of a civilization. The man who keeps form present already performs the residual act. That act does not require a collective horizon to remain real.

Maintain form without collective support. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions in which higher forms are publicly sustained. The residual act is to keep the permanent measure effective in a world that systematically dissolves every vertical structure. That effectiveness is the continuity of the higher type.
