Day 354 — Bearing Without Witness

"Style is the visible sign of an inner law that does not depend on the approval of the surrounding world." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

Bearing without witness is the outer form of an inner rank that no longer has a court, a caste, or a public order to display it. Bearing is how a man occupies space, how he speaks, how he moves through fatigue and appetite. It is not costume. Costume is put on for others. Bearing continues when others are absent because it is the body of an interior law.

Young men are offered two cheap substitutes: slovenliness dressed as honesty, and display dressed as excellence. Both are horizontal. The first dissolves form. The second rents form from an audience. Traditional bearing is quieter. It is limit made visible in posture, dress, and voice without asking to be praised for the limit.

This solitary bearing is the residual form of the older traditional knowledge of dignity. Dignity once had a public language of rank. It now has only the man himself. The man who keeps clean form without a gallery already performs the residual act.

Carry bearing without witness. Do not slacken because no one of rank is present to notice. The residual act is to let the body continue to speak a higher law in a world that no longer speaks that language. The body will remember what the age wants forgotten.
