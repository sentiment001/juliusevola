Day 260 — Standing as Residual Continuity

"To remain standing is already the residual form of higher continuity when every collective expression of that continuity has been dissolved." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

Standing as residual continuity continues after every collective posture that once expressed higher standing has been leveled. The residual man continues to remain upright according to the permanent measure even though the age offers no confirming rank and no shared recognition. The standing itself remains intact. Only the collective structures that once made it visible have disappeared.

Young men who still sense the need to stand often look for residual movements or residual ranks that will give their posture external weight. Most of those movements have already been reshaped by the horizontal forces that dominate the age. The residual path does not wait for favorable conditions. It continues to stand from the interior center that remains available.

This solitary standing is the residual form of the older traditional posture that once ordered both the individual and the higher functions of a civilization. The man who keeps standing already performs the residual act of continuity. That act does not require a collective horizon to remain real.

Stand as residual continuity. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions in which higher standing is publicly recognized. The residual act is to keep the permanent measure effective in a world that systematically levels every vertical posture. That effectiveness is the continuity of the higher type.
