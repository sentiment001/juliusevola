Day 256 — The Type That Continues

"The higher type does not require the survival of the civilization that once carried it. It continues wherever a single man still forms himself according to the permanent criteria." — Julius Evola, Revolt Against the Modern World

The type that continues does so after every collective form that once transmitted it has been leveled. The residual man continues to form himself according to the permanent criteria even though the age offers no confirmation and no favorable terrain. The type remains the same. Only the collective environment that once transmitted it has disappeared.

Young men who still sense the higher type often look for residual environments or residual groups that will make formation less solitary. Most of those environments have already been shaped by the horizontal forces that dominate the age. The residual path does not wait for favorable conditions. It continues the formation of the type from the interior center that remains available.

This independent formation is the residual form of the older traditional process that once ordered the higher castes across generations. The man who keeps forming the type already performs the residual act of continuity. That act does not require a collective horizon to remain real.

Form the type that continues. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions favorable to higher formation. The residual act is to keep the permanent criteria effective in a world that has no place for them. That effectiveness is the continuity of the higher type.
