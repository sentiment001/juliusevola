Day 244 — Vertical Orientation Without Support

"Orientation remains vertical when it is fixed by a criterion that does not depend on any external support the age is still able to provide." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

Vertical orientation without support continues after every external structure that once sustained vertical direction has been removed. The residual man continues to orient himself according to the permanent criterion even though the age offers no shared axis and no collective confirmation. The orientation itself remains unbroken. Only the public conditions that once made it visible have disappeared.

Young men who still seek a stable direction often look for residual maps or residual teachers that will orient them from outside. Most of those maps have already been redrawn to match the requirements of the age. The residual path does not wait for external orientation. It maintains the permanent criterion from the interior pole that remains accessible.

This unsupported orientation is the residual form of the older traditional axis that once connected the individual to higher orders of being. The man who keeps it effective already carries the residual polarity. That polarity does not require a collective horizon to remain real.

Hold vertical orientation without support. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions in which vertical direction is shared. The residual act is to keep the fixed criterion present in a world that moves only horizontally. That presence is the continuity of the higher type.
