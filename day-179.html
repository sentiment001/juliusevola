Day 179 — The Residual Measure

"When every external support has fallen away, the measure itself must be carried by the man who still recognizes it. That carrying is the residual form of the higher order." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

The measure that remains is the permanent standard the differentiated man still carries after the supports have disappeared. In an earlier order that standard lived in institutions, rites, and a hierarchy other men could see. Those carriers are gone. What remains must be held interiorly and applied where no office will confirm the application. The man who continues to apply it keeps the higher orientation present.

This measure stays what it was when ordered worlds still stood. It does not bend to the average condition of the room. The path consists in the repeated decision to judge and act by that unchanged standard under conditions that punish it. The decision has to be made again after every easy day. Each repetition is the continuity of the type.

Young men who meet this idea look for a lighter version that will fit the company they want to keep. The lighter version is the surrender wearing a practical face. Advancement, belonging, and a quieter evening are the payments offered for the discount. A friend will call the original line arrogance, and a role will treat it as a private taste to be left at the door. The friction that follows from refusing the discount is the price of remaining vertical.

Hold the standard without dilution on the next decision that asks for a discount. How the body is used tonight, what is said to fit the room, what is accepted because the job rewards only output: each is a place the measure is carried or set down. Absorbing the friction without a speech is part of the carrying. The man who keeps the permanent standard active in his own decisions already carries what an older world once carried in public.
