Day 263 — The Man of Residual Rank

"Rank of a higher order does not disappear when the external hierarchy that once assigned it has been destroyed. It continues in the man who still carries it as an interior reality." — Julius Evola, Revolt Against the Modern World

The man of residual rank continues after every external hierarchy that once assigned higher rank has been leveled. The residual man continues to carry rank according to the permanent criteria even though the age offers no confirming position and no shared recognition. The rank itself remains intact. Only the collective structures that once made it visible have disappeared.

Young men who still sense the need for rank often look for residual hierarchies or residual titles that will assign it from outside. Most of those hierarchies have already been reshaped by the horizontal forces that dominate the age. The residual path does not wait for external assignment. It continues to carry rank from the interior center that still perceives the permanent order.

This solitary rank is the residual form of the older traditional hierarchy that once ordered both the individual and the higher functions of a civilization. The man who keeps rank present already performs the residual act. That act does not require a collective horizon to remain real.

Carry residual rank. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions in which higher rank is publicly assigned. The residual act is to keep the permanent criteria effective in a world that systematically levels every vertical distinction. That effectiveness is the continuity of the higher type.
