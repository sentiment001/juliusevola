Day 280 — Orientation That Does Not Dissolve

"Orientation that survives is orientation that never required the survival of collective axes for its force." — Julius Evola, Revolt Against the Modern World

Orientation that does not dissolve continues after every collective orientation of higher order has been dissolved. The residual man continues the permanent orientation even though the age offers no confirming direction and no shared axis. The orientation itself remains intact. Only the collective structures that once supported it have disappeared.

Young men who still seek direction often look for residual axes or residual communities that will supply the orientation from outside. Most of those communities have already been reshaped by the horizontal forces that dominate the age. The residual path does not wait for external orientation. It continues from the interior center that still holds the vertical measure.

This solitary orientation is the residual form of the older traditional orientation that once ordered both the individual and the civilization. The man who keeps the orientation present already performs the residual act. That act does not require a collective axis to remain real.

Maintain orientation that does not dissolve. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions in which higher direction is publicly structured. The residual act is to keep the permanent measure effective in every concrete decision. That effectiveness is the continuity of the higher type under residual conditions.
