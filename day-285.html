Day 285 — The Interior Axis Alone

"The axis that remains is the one the man maintains within himself when every external axis has been removed." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

The interior axis alone continues after every collective axis of higher order has been broken. The residual man continues to orient himself according to the permanent center even though the age offers no confirming direction and no shared pole. The axis itself remains intact. Only the collective structures that once supported it have disappeared.

Young men who still seek orientation often look for residual poles or residual communities that will supply the axis from outside. Most of those communities have already been reshaped by the horizontal forces that dominate the age. The residual path does not wait for external orientation. It continues from the interior center that still holds the vertical measure.

This solitary axis is the residual form of the older traditional axis that once ordered both the individual and the civilization. The man who keeps the axis present already performs the residual act. That act does not require a collective pole to remain real.

Maintain the interior axis alone. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions in which higher orientation is publicly structured. The residual act is to keep the permanent measure effective in every concrete decision. That effectiveness is the continuity of the higher type under residual conditions.
