Day 211 — The Residual Center

"The center must now be established within the individual himself, since the external supports of Tradition have withdrawn from the world." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

The external supports that once oriented the higher type have withdrawn. What remains is the possibility of a center that no longer depends on collective forms for its existence. This residual center is not a private invention. It is the same vertical axis that once ordered traditional civilizations, now carried by the man who is capable of holding it alone.

Young men feel the absence of external orientation as a continuous pressure toward either collapse or theatrical reconstruction. The residual path refuses both. The man who establishes the center within himself already occupies the position that the cycle still permits. That position requires no collective confirmation to remain real.

The residual center is tested by every force that seeks to dissolve it. The age offers continuous invitations to abandon the vertical measure and to live according to the horizontal criteria that now dominate. The differentiated individual meets those invitations with the same refusal. He continues to orient himself by the permanent criterion even when that criterion finds no echo outside him.

Carry the residual center. Do not wait for the age to restore the structures that once made the center a collective reality. The man who keeps it present already performs the residual act. That act is the continuity of the higher type under conditions that no longer favor it.
