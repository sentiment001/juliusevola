Day 73 — The Higher Continuity

"What matters is that a continuity of the higher type be maintained across the break in the outer forms. The differentiated individual is the bearer of that continuity when every institutional support has fallen away." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

The higher continuity is the transmission of a quality of being from one generation to the next even when the structures that once guaranteed it no longer exist. In Traditional orders this continuity was carried by blood, by initiation, by the living presence of caste and rite. Those channels have been interrupted. The differentiated individual becomes the temporary vessel. He keeps the vertical orientation alive in his own person so that it does not vanish entirely from the world.

This is a heavy responsibility and a limited one. The man does not imagine himself as the founder of a new order or the restorer of the old. He simply refuses to allow the higher measure to die in him. The refusal itself is the continuity. If enough such men persist, something of the type remains available for whatever phase may follow the present dissolution. The outcome is not guaranteed. The duty is clear regardless of the outcome.

Most of the surrounding culture works against this continuity. Education, media, and the economic order all favor the production of interchangeable units. The differentiated man stands as a break in that production. His existence is already a form of resistance that does not require public declaration. The age will try to absorb him or to pathologize the firmness he maintains. He continues anyway.

The higher continuity is not nostalgia. It does not live in the reconstruction of costumes or the imitation of past gestures. It lives in the actual presence of a man who still measures himself by a standard the age has forgotten. That presence is rare. Its rarity does not diminish its necessity. The work of the remaining days of this phase is to make that presence more stable and more complete.
