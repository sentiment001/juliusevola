Day 264 — Clarity in the Final Phase

"Clarity of a higher order does not vanish in the final phase of a cycle. It continues in the man who still sees according to the permanent measure when every collective clarity has been extinguished." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

Clarity in the final phase continues after every collective clarity that once ordered higher life has been dissolved. The residual man continues to see according to the permanent orientation even though the age offers no confirming light and no shared vision. The clarity itself remains intact. Only the collective structures that once made it common have disappeared.

Young men who still feel the need for clarity often look for residual teachers or residual doctrines that will supply it from outside. Most of those doctrines have already absorbed the horizontal logic of the age. The residual path does not wait for external illumination. It continues to see from the interior center that still perceives the permanent axis.

This solitary clarity is the residual form of the older traditional vision that once ordered both the individual and the higher functions of a civilization. The man who keeps clarity present already performs the residual act. That act does not require a collective horizon to remain real.

Maintain clarity in the final phase. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions in which higher vision is publicly available. The residual act is to keep the permanent measure effective in a world that systematically extinguishes every vertical light. That effectiveness is the continuity of the higher type.
