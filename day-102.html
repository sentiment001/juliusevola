Day 102 — Work

"Work in the modern sense has been stripped of any higher meaning. For the differentiated individual it must be restored as a domain of order and of self-mastery, even when the outer form of the work remains determined by the conditions of the age." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

Work is the field where most of a young man's waking hours are spent. The age has reduced it to economic function, to the exchange of time for money, to a necessary burden whose only justification is the consumption it enables. That reduction is part of the larger dissolution. The differentiated individual cannot accept the reduction as final. He must find a way to perform the work that his circumstances require while keeping the higher measure intact inside it.

This does not mean inventing a romantic meaning for every task. Many forms of modern work are genuinely empty of higher content. The demand is more severe. The man must bring an interior order to the performance itself. Precision, reliability, the refusal of the casual and the half-done, the capacity to sustain attention without the continuous stimulation the age provides: these are applications of the same principle that governs the rest of the type. The outer task may be determined by the economic order. The quality of the presence brought to it is not.

Young men are taught to treat work either as the path to status or as an obstacle to freedom. Both attitudes keep the man inside the horizontal measure. The differentiated individual works because the higher type does not permit disorder in the domain under his direct control. He also works because dependence on the favor of the age is a form of surrender. Competence that can be exercised under pressure is a form of independence. The friction with the surrounding culture is constant. Most will interpret seriousness about ordinary work as careerism or as a lack of higher ambition. The higher type holds the distinction without explaining it to those who cannot see it.

Apply the permanent measure to the hours of work. Refuse the habits of distraction and of minimal effort that the environment rewards. The body of work a man produces over years is one of the visible records of the type he has chosen to become. Let that record be consistent with the orientation already established.
