Day 229 — The Man of Permanent Orientation

"Orientation is permanent when it is fixed by a criterion that does not change with the phases of the cycle. The differentiated man holds that criterion when every external compass has been destroyed." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

The man of permanent orientation continues to move according to a fixed vertical criterion while the age accelerates in every horizontal direction. The residual man does not adjust his direction to the prevailing currents. He keeps the same orientation that once ordered traditional civilizations from the center outward.

Young men who still seek a stable direction often look for residual maps or residual teachers that will orient them from outside. Most of those maps have already been redrawn to match the requirements of the age. The residual path does not wait for external orientation. It maintains the permanent criterion from the interior pole that remains accessible.

This permanent orientation is the residual form of the older traditional axis that once connected the individual to higher orders of being. The man who keeps it effective already carries the residual polarity. That polarity does not require a collective horizon to remain real.

Hold the permanent orientation. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions in which vertical direction is shared. The residual act is to keep the fixed criterion present in a world that moves only horizontally. That presence is the continuity of the higher type.
