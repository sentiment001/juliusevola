Day 290 — Residual Sovereignty of Orientation

"Sovereignty of orientation does not depend on the survival of a world that once shared it. It continues in the man who still determines his direction from the interior pole." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

Residual sovereignty of orientation is the capacity to keep the vertical direction intact after every collective orientation of higher order has collapsed. The residual man continues to orient himself from the permanent center even though the age supplies no confirming horizon. The sovereignty itself remains intact. Only the collective coordinates that once made orientation shared have disappeared.

Young men who still seek a clear direction often look for residual maps or residual authorities that will supply the orientation from outside. Most of those maps have already been redrawn by the horizontal forces that dominate the age. The residual path does not wait for external coordinates. It continues as the permanent direction from the interior pole that still holds the vertical measure.

This solitary sovereignty is the residual form of the older traditional capacity to orient an entire civilization. The man who continues to determine his direction from the center already performs the residual act. That act does not require a shared horizon to remain real.

Orient yourself. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions in which higher direction is publicly available. The residual act is to keep the permanent orientation decisive in every concrete movement. That decisiveness is the continuity of the higher type under residual conditions.
