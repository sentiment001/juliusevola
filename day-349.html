Day 349 — The Seed of the Higher Order

"What matters is that a certain type remains standing, so that when the cycle turns there is still a seed of the higher order." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

The seed of the higher order is a living type who still obeys a vertical measure when the surrounding world has forgotten that such a measure exists. A seed is not a plan stored in a notebook. It is a man whose body, speech, and interior law still belong to a higher form of life. If that man disappears, the cycle has nothing left to turn toward.

Young men treat ideas as seeds. They collect doctrines and imagine that the collection itself preserves something. Doctrine without a type is paper. The age already produces endless paper. What it cannot produce is a man who remains inwardly ranked when every outer rank has dissolved. That man is the only continuity Tradition still has in a late phase.

This solitary conservation of the type is the residual form of the older traditional knowledge of transmission. Transmission once moved through caste, rite, and initiation. It now moves, if it moves at all, through the reality of one life held under a higher law. The man who accepts that narrow channel already performs the residual act.

Become the seed. Do not wait for a climate that will grow you. The residual act is to keep the higher type real in one body and one conduct until the phase changes or until you end. That reality is the only inheritance this age can still receive.
