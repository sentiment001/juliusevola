Day 228 — What Survives the Cycle

"At the end of a cycle what survives is not the institutions or the collective forms, but the possibility of the higher type continuing to form itself according to the same eternal criteria." — Julius Evola, Revolt Against the Modern World

What survives the cycle is the type itself when the type has been formed according to the permanent measure. The residual man continues to embody that type even though every collective form that once supported it has been dissolved. Survival here is not a matter of numbers or institutions. It is the persistence of a vertical orientation inside a single man.

Young men who sense the end of the present forms often look for residual lineages or residual groups that will guarantee the survival of the higher type. Most of what they find has already been shaped by the horizontal forces that dominate the age. The residual path does not depend on such guarantees. It continues the formation of the type from the interior center that remains available.

This solitary survival is the residual form of the older traditional continuity that once ordered entire civilizations across generations. The man who keeps the type present already performs the residual act of survival. That act does not require a collective horizon to remain real.

Form the type that survives. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions favorable to its collective existence. The residual act is to keep the permanent criteria effective in a world that has no place for them. That effectiveness is the only survival that still belongs to the higher type.
