A daily reader drawn from the core of Julius Evola's work. Written for young men who feel the narrowness of the age they were born into and want a clearer standard.

These pages take their main direction from Revolt Against the Modern World and Ride the Tiger. The early days establish orientation. Later days will move into diagnosis and practical stance.
