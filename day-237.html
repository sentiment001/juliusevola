Day 237 — Permanent Rank Without Position

"Rank is permanent when it is rooted in the quality of the type rather than in any temporary position within a social hierarchy. The differentiated man carries that rank when every external rank has been abolished." — Julius Evola, Revolt Against the Modern World

Permanent rank continues after every external hierarchy that once expressed it has been leveled. The residual man continues to occupy that rank from the interior even though the age recognizes no higher place and no higher function. Rank remains real because it is grounded in the permanent measure, not in the temporary arrangements of a civilization.

Young men who still sense higher rank often look for residual positions or residual groups that will grant them external standing. Most of those positions have already been emptied of vertical content by the forces of the age. The residual path does not wait for external rank. It maintains the permanent rank from the quality of the type itself.

This interior rank is the residual form of the older traditional hierarchy that once ordered both individual and civilization according to quality. The man who keeps it effective already carries the residual distinction. That distinction does not require collective acknowledgment to remain binding.

Carry permanent rank. Do not wait for the age to restore hierarchies capable of expressing higher quality. The residual act is to keep the permanent criterion of rank present in a world that systematically levels every height. That presence is the continuity of the higher type.
