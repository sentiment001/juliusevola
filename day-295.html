Day 295 — Orientation That Survives Dissolution

"Orientation survives the dissolution of every collective horizon. It continues in the man who still determines his direction from the interior pole." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

Orientation that survives dissolution is the residual capacity to keep the vertical direction after every collective orientation of higher order has been erased. The residual man continues to orient himself from the permanent center even though the age supplies no confirming coordinates. The orientation itself remains intact. Only the collective coordinates that once made direction shared have disappeared.

Young men who still seek a clear direction often look for residual maps or residual authorities that will supply the orientation from outside. Most of those maps have already been redrawn by the horizontal forces that dominate the age. The residual path does not wait for external coordinates. It continues as the permanent direction from the interior pole that still holds the vertical measure.

This solitary orientation is the residual form of the older traditional capacity to orient an entire civilization. The man who continues to determine his direction from the center already performs the residual act. That act does not require a shared horizon to remain real.

Orient yourself. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions in which higher direction is publicly available. The residual act is to keep the permanent orientation decisive in every concrete movement. That decisiveness is the continuity of the higher type under residual conditions.
