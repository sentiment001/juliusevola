Day 226 — The Residual Demand

"The higher demand does not disappear when the civilization that once embodied it has fallen. It continues as an interior imperative for those still capable of hearing it." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

The residual demand remains in force after every collective structure that once enforced it has collapsed. The residual man continues to answer that demand even though the age no longer recognizes its legitimacy. The demand itself is permanent. Only the external conditions that once made obedience to it a shared duty have vanished.

Young men who still feel the pressure of a higher standard often search for residual groups or residual authorities that will make the demand easier to carry. Most of what they encounter has already compromised the standard in order to survive inside the age. The residual path does not wait for such support. It answers the demand from the interior center that still perceives it.

This solitary answer is the residual form of the older traditional obligation that once ordered the higher castes. The man who continues to answer already performs the residual duty. That duty does not become less binding because it is no longer collective.

Answer the residual demand. Do not wait for the age to restore the structures that once made the higher measure a public reality. The residual act is to keep the permanent imperative present in a world that has abolished every external reminder of it. That presence is the continuity of the higher type under residual conditions.
