Day 162 — The Vertical in the Horizontal Age

"The vertical orientation does not disappear because the age has become horizontal. It becomes a residual possibility that the differentiated individual must realize from within." — Julius Evola, Revolt Against the Modern World

The vertical in the horizontal age is the residual presence of the higher dimension under conditions that systematically deny it. The horizontal age measures everything by quantity, by sensation, by the collective level. The vertical measure continues to exist as the permanent standard that the residual type applies to himself. The application does not require the age to become vertical again. It requires only the capacity of the man.

The horizontal pressure is constant. It appears in the demand for equality of outcome, in the reduction of every higher claim to private preference, and in the continuous stimulation that keeps attention on the surface. The residual type meets the pressure by maintaining the interior hierarchy. Rank is reestablished inside the man even when it has been abolished outside him.

Young men feel the horizontal pressure as the air they breathe. The residual path does not remove them from the air. It trains them to breathe it without allowing it to rewrite the measure. The training is the daily return to the residual axis under ordinary conditions. Each return strengthens the vertical residue.

Keep the vertical under horizontal pressure. Do not wait for the age to change its direction. The man who maintains the residual orientation is already living the vertical possibility that the sequence has been clarifying. That living is the residual form of the traditional hierarchy.
