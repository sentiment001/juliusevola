Day 332 — Residual Continuity of Orientation

"Continuity of orientation does not require the collective sequences that once sustained it. It continues as the residual direction of the man who still holds the measure." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

Residual continuity of orientation is the capacity to keep the higher direction unbroken after every collective orientation of higher order has been interrupted. The residual man continues that continuity even though the age offers no confirming sequence. The continuity itself remains intact. Only the collective sequences that once made its force public have disappeared.

Young men who still sense the need for unbroken direction often look for residual sequences or residual traditions that will keep the continuity alive around them. Most of those traditions have already been emptied by the horizontal forces that dominate the age. The residual path does not wait for external sequence. It continues as the permanent continuity from the interior center that still holds the vertical measure.

This solitary continuity is the residual form of the older traditional knowledge of the unbroken way. The man who continues it already performs the residual act. That act does not require collective sequences to remain real.

Keep the continuity. Do not wait for the age to restore conditions in which higher orientation is collectively sustained. The residual act is to keep the permanent measure decisive in every concrete step. That decisiveness is the continuity of the higher type under residual conditions.
