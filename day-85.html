Day 85 — What Remains Possible

"In the conditions of the end of a cycle, what remains possible is the preservation of the higher type in individuals. That preservation is already a victory. Everything else is secondary." — Julius Evola, Ride the Tiger

What remains possible is limited and precise. The restoration of a full Traditional order is not among the immediate possibilities. The conversion of the mass is not among them. The reversal of the cycle by human effort is not among them. What remains is the continued existence of men who still embody the vertical orientation, who still measure themselves by a standard the age has abandoned, and who are still capable of riding the forces of dissolution without being dissolved by them.

This limitation is not a counsel of despair. It is a clarification of the real field of action. Energy spent on impossible goals is energy lost to the only goal that is still open. The differentiated individual therefore concentrates. He works on the formation and the maintenance of his own type. He does what is within his reach to keep the higher measure alive in the concrete conditions of his life. That work is modest in appearance. Its significance is not measured by appearance.

Young men who feel the weight of the age often look for a larger and more dramatic possibility. They want a movement, a restoration, a visible turning of the tide. The desire is understandable. When it becomes the condition for any serious orientation, it becomes a form of self-sabotage. The man waits for conditions that may not arrive in his lifetime and in the waiting he fails to become the type that would still matter if those conditions did arrive.

What remains possible is therefore also what is required. The man becomes what the age no longer produces. He remains standing while the current runs. He rides the tiger with the clarity and the detachment that the previous days have examined. If that much is achieved, something essential has been preserved. The rest belongs to the cycle itself and to whatever may follow it.
